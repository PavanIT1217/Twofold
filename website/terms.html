What Twofold is

Twofold is an app for keeping your family's important information in an encrypted vault on your own devices, and sharing it with family members you pair with.

Your responsibilities

  • Keep your master password and printed recovery kit safe. We can't recover a vault for you, because we never hold your keys or data.
  • Keep your phone's own screen lock turned on and only pair with people you trust. Paired members can see the shared vault according to their role.
  • Keep backups. Turn on the encrypted Google Drive backup, or save an encrypted backup file now and then.
  • Use Twofold lawfully, and only for information you have the right to store.

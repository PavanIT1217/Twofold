How it works

Three steps. No cloud account with us.

Twofold trusts exactly one thing: the phones in your family's hands.

Create your vault

Choose a master password. Twofold prints you a recovery kit to keep with your property papers or in your bank locker.

Pair in person

Scan each other's QR codes and check that the numbers match. Only phones you've paired this way can ever open your vault.

Sync at home

On the same Wi-Fi, your phones swap changes directly and encrypted. Each phone can also keep an encrypted backup in its own Google Drive.

Our promise

Private by construction, not by policy.

We didn't just promise not to look. We built Twofold so there's nothing for us to look at.

What you get

  • Encryption on your phone before anything is stored or sent.
  • Only phones you paired in person can open the vault.
  • Encrypted backups in your Google Drive, or a backup file you keep.
  • Every item, document and note works fully offline.

What we don't have

  • No servers holding your data.
  • No Twofold accounts, emails or phone numbers.
  • No analytics, ads or tracking. Not even on this website.
  • No way to see, recover or hand over your vault.
Questions

Good to know.

What if I forget my master password?

Use the recovery kit you printed when you set up Twofold. Another paired family member can also still open the shared vault. We can't reset it for you, because we never have your keys. That's what keeps your data private.

What if I lose my phone?

The vault is locked with your master password and fingerprint or face unlock, so whoever finds the phone can't read it. On your new phone, restore the encrypted backup from your Google Drive or a backup file, or pair again with a family member's phone.

Do we have to be at home to sync?

Your phones sync when they're on the same Wi-Fi network. Everyone can always open their own copy anytime, anywhere, even offline. Changes reach the other phones the next time you're together on Wi-Fi.

Can more than two people share a vault?

Yes. Twofold is made for couples first, and you can add up to six family members, each with their own role.

Does it work between Android and iPhone?

Yes. An Android phone and an iPhone can pair and sync with each other.

How it works

No sign-up, no cloud account with us, nothing to trust except the phones in your hands.

1

Create your vault

Pick a master password. Twofold makes a recovery kit you print and keep with your important papers.

2

Pair in person

Scan each other's QR code once. That swaps the keys that let only your phones open the vault. Add up to six family members.

3

Stay in sync at home

When your phones are on the same Wi-Fi, they swap changes directly and encrypted. Each phone also keeps an encrypted backup in its own Google Drive or iCloud.

Questions

What if I forget my master password?

Use the recovery kit you printed when you set up Twofold. Another paired family member can also still open the shared vault. We can't reset it for you, because we never have your keys. That's what keeps your data private.

What if I lose my phone?

The vault is locked with your master password and fingerprint or face unlock, so whoever finds the phone can't read it. On your new phone, restore the encrypted backup from your Google Drive or iCloud, or pair again with your partner's phone.

Do we have to be at home to sync?

Your phones sync when they're on the same Wi-Fi network. Everyone can always open their own copy anytime, anywhere, even offline. Changes reach the other phones the next time you're together on Wi-Fi.

Can more than two people share a vault?

Yes. Twofold is made for couples first, and you can add up to six family members, each with their own role.

Does it work between Android and iPhone?

Yes. An Android phone and an iPhone can pair and sync with each other.
